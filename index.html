Welcome to Nyankpala Zone, YPG Portal!

Nyankpala Zone, YPG! We warmly welcome you to our official digital home. We bring together vibrant Christian youths dedicated and committed to building a greater community grounded in faith, deep fellowship, and active service to Christ.

Young People's Guild (YPG) Logo

The Young People's Guild is youth fellowship of the Presbyterian Church of Ghana, committed to nurturing young Christians in faith, fellowship, and service to God and humanity.
The Young People's Guild seek to promote the moral and social well being of its members. It serves as a platform for members to realize their potentials, opportunities and responsibilities within the church and the community. Join us as we work together to make disciples of all nations and promote the spiritual growth of young people in the Presbyterian Church of Ghana.
