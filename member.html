    Member of the Month

    NOVEMBER 2026

    ALHASSAN FUSEINI PHILIP

    Meet ALHASSAN FUSEINI PHILIP, a dedicated member of the Presbyterian Young People's Guild whose journey reflects faith, commitment, fellowship, and service. Through active participation in church and YPG activities, he continues to inspire others with a heart for God and a passion for serving others. "Worship to the Lord is not about what you want to use yourself to do, but what the Lord Jesus wants you to do!" We celebrate you! Bro Philip, for your dedicated and contribution to the growth of the Guild. May God continue to strengthen, guide, and use you for His glory.

    Faith Journey

    I was born to Muslim parents, but some of my uncles and brothers were Christians. Growing up, I often followed my brothers and other boys in the house to church. This became part of my early exposure to Christianity and the church.
    I have represented Freedom Congregation-Kpana in Seed Ministry quiz competitions, and my best position was 2nd place.

    There was also a period in my life when I became a Muslim. During that time, I would call people to prayer and sometimes lead prayers, especially Maghrib and Shaf'ir Witr, in the mosque. Interestingly, on my way to the mosque, I would often be singing Gospel songs until Ireached the mosque.

    One day, however, I just felt that I should go back to church. I started attending church occasionally. Whenever people asked me why I was not attending regularly, I would say it was because of my father, even though my father had not actually told me not to attend church. This continued until one of my uncles asked my father about it. My father then made it clear that he had no problem with me going to church. When he said that, I started going to church so that people would no longer ask me about it. But I truly became a born-again Christian when I personally studied the Bible and began to understand certain things for myself. That was an important turning point in my faith journey.

    YPG Experience

    I started following my uncles and brothers to church programmes such as night services, all-night services, Bible studies, prayers, and other activities. As I grew older, I started attending YPG Camps, where I sometimes participated in quizzes and other activities. I have also represented the Zone and the District in quiz competitions.
    These experiences have given me opportunities to learn, serve, and grow within the Guild. Currently, I serve as the Assistant General Secretary of the Nyankpala Zone YPG, where I continue to contribute to the work and growth of the Guild.

    Looking back at my journey, I am grateful for how God has led me through different experiences. From becoming a Muslim myself for a period, and eventually coming to know Christ personally. I believe this was God's direction and mercy.

    Nyankpala Zone Guild Celebrate You!!
    Bro. Alhassan Fuseini Philip, may the Lord continue to strengthen you, increase your understanding of His Word, and guide you as you serve Him and His people.
