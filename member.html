NOVEMBER 2026

ALHASSAN FUSEINI PHILIP

Meet ALHASSAN FUSEINI PHILIP, a dedicated member of the Presbyterian Young People's Guild whose journey reflects faith, commitment, fellowship, and service. Through active participation in church and YPG activities, he continues to inspire others with a heart for God and a passion for serving others. "Worship to the Lord is not about what you want to use yourself to do, but what the Lord Jesus wants you to do!" We celebrate you! Bro Philip, for your dedicated and contribution to the growth of the Guild. May God continue to strengthen, guide, and use you for His glory.

Faith Journey

Philip was born to a muslim parents, but some of the uncles and brothers were christians. So growing up he use to follow his brothers and the other house boys to church most of the times. He has represented the church (Freedom Congregation-Kpana) in Seed Ministry quiz competetions and his best possition was 2nd. There was a time i became a muslim, i use to call for prayers and some times lead prayers especially Magrib and Shafir wutri in the mosque. One interesting thing was that, always on my way to the Mosque, i use to be singing Gospel songs until i reach the Mosque. But one day i just feeled i should go back to the church, then i started going to Church rarely. So when they asked me why i'm not puntual, i said its because of my father when my father didn't actually say anything. It was like that until one of my uncles asked my father and he said he doesn't have any problem with me going to church. When he said that i started going so that they wouldn't ask me again. But i actually became a born againg child when i studied the Bible and understood some things myself.

YPG Experience

We started following our uncles and brothers to night service such as all night services, bible study, prayers and others. When our eyes opened small, we started atendding YPG Camps where i some times participate in quizes and other stuffs. I also represented the Zone and the district in quizes. Currently i'm the assistant General Secretary for Nyankpala Zone YPG.
