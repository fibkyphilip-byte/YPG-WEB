    Who We Are

    Purpose & Direction

    👁

    OUR VISION

    To see a generation of young Presbyterians who are spiritually grounded, socially responsible, and actively engaged in transforming their communities for Christ.

    🎯

    OUR MISSION

    To nurture the spiritual growth of young people within the Presbyterian Church of Ghana especially those around Nyankpala, equipping them with the zeal to serve God and humanity.

    OUR HISTORY

    The Young People's Guild (YPG) was founded in 1938 by Andrews McCutcheon Atkinson, a missionary of the Basel Mission who recognized the need to engage and nurture the youth of the Presbyterian Church of Ghana.
    From its humble beginnings, the YPG has grown into one of the most vibrant youth organizations within the Presbyterian Church of Ghana, providing young people with opportunities for spiritual growth, leadership, fellowship, and service.

    In the Nyankpala Zone, the first executives were elected in 2004 during the tenure of Rev. Nagba, at a gathering held at Tingoli. Since then, the Zone has had several successive administrations, with notable leadership transitions in 2015, 2018, 2019, 2022, and 2025.

    Over the years, the Nyankpala Zone YPG has organized and participated in significant activities, including conventions, retreats, conferences, and all-night services. One notable event was the 2023 Zone YPG Convention held at T. Cheshegu.

    Through the dedication of successive leaders and members, the YPG continues to grow in faith, fellowship, leadership, and service.
